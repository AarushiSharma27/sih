Consent

Plain language, because consent you did not understand is not consent

What information will be collected?

Your registration details; your attendance, assessment score and certificate; and — at 3, 6, 12 and 24 months after training — whether you are working, what you do, who you work for and what you earn.

Why is it being collected?

So the programme can tell whether training actually led to a livelihood. Today the system stops measuring at certification, which is why courses that do not lead to jobs can run unchanged for years.

How will it be used?

Published figures are always aggregated — by course, provider, district or cohort. Your name, phone number and salary are never shown on any dashboard. An employer sees only the record of the person they hired. You can withdraw consent at any time, and you can ask for a copy of everything held about you.
