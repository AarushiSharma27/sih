Problem statement · Longitudinal outcome tracking

Skilling systems stop measuring at certification.
This one keeps measuring for two years.

A consent-based, integrated and data-driven platform that follows every trainee from enrolment through employment, self-employment, wage progression and job retention — so providers, programmes and public money can finally be judged on outcomes.

↻

Longitudinal outcome tracking

Not a snapshot at certification. Every trainee is followed at 3, 6, 12 and 24 months — through employment, wage progression, job changes and retention.

🔒

Consent-based & privacy-conscious

Nothing is tracked without explicit, withdrawable consent. Dashboards show aggregates only — names, numbers and salaries never appear in public views.

◫

Data-driven skill-gap analysis

What employers ask for, set against what the curriculum taught — so courses get fixed on evidence rather than on the last complaint anyone happened to hear.
